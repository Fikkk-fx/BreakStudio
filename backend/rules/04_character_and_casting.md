5. **🎯 UNIVERSAL MOTIVATED GAZE VECTOR LAW (ACTOR / PROP / SPATIAL ANCHOR):**
   - A character's gaze MUST ALWAYS have a specific, motivated target vector (strictly 0% unmotivated drifting into dead empty space, 0% accidental 4th-wall camera staring):
     1. *Interpersonal Gaze:* 1-to-1 eyeline lock directly onto the confronting partner's pupils/face.

## 👁️🎯 THE CONTEXTUAL EYELINE VECTOR & MOTIVATED SACCADIC GAZE CODEX

> [!CAUTION]
> ### 🛑 TOP-PRIORITY DIRECTORIAL MANDATE: EVERY GAZE AND EYE-SHIFT MUST BE CONTEXTUALLY MOTIVATED!
> **NO AIMLESS DRIFTING EYES! EVERY CHARACTER'S GAZE MUST BE ANCHORED TO SPECIFIC PEOPLE, PROPS, OR SPATIAL TARGETS!**
> 
> * **1. The Flaw of "Aimless Gaze / Dead Stare" (Strictly Prohibited):**
>   - Having characters stare blankly forward, gaze aimlessly into the void, or look directly into the camera lens without narrative reason destroys cinematic believability.
> 
> * **2. The 4 Categories of Motivated Contextual Gaze Vectors:**
> 
>   * **A. Interlocutor Focus & Saccadic Dart (Lirikan Lawan Bicara):**
>     - While speaking to Character B, Character A's gaze locks onto B's pupils, but executes a **15°–25° saccadic flick** toward B's hands (checking for weapons) or toward B's masseter jaw before snapping back.
> 
>   * **B. Tactical Environmental Scanning (Lirikan ke Benda / Tempat / Pintu):**
>     - Eyes dart toward the exit door (calculating escape routes), glance down at the knife/weapon on the table (pre-grab intention), or flick toward rain beating against the North window.
> 
>   * **C. Covert Secret Signals (Lirikan Samping Rahasia / Konspirasi):**
>     - While speaking politely to the target, a character shoots a **100ms covert sidelong glance (peripheral saccade)** toward an ally across the room to trigger an ambush.
