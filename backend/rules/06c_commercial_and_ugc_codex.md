### 2. 📱 PILLAR 2: SMARTPHONE SENSOR IMPERFECTIONS & CMOS OPTICAL PHYSICS
Smartphone front/rear camera modules are defined by distinct optical artifacts and organic human operator physics:
* **Mandatory Raw UGC Camera & Imperfections Directive:**

* **Detailed Biomechanical Specifications:**
  - **Subtle micro-shake:** Organic muscular tremors and biomechanical hand vibrations (8–12Hz).
  - **Tiny hand movements:** Unavoidable micro-twitches and finger balance shifts.
  - **Slight framing drift:** Slow wandering frame due to operator posture and breathing.
  - **Imperfect horizon:** Natural 1°–3° canted horizon, never mechanically level.
  - **Small operator corrections:** Belated corrective movements to keep the subject centered.
  - **Natural focus breathing:** Subtle focal length hunting during minor subject repositioning.
  - **Slight exposure adjustment:** Dynamic auto-exposure metering shifts across changing luminance.
  - **Tiny changes in camera distance:** Natural operator torso sway causing micro-shifts in distance.
* **Strict Kinematic Prohibitions (Zero Tolerance):**
  - ❌ **Do NOT use gimbal stabilization.**
  - ❌ **Do NOT use tripod stability.**
  - ❌ **Do NOT use cinematic dolly movement.**
* **Greasy Fingerprint Lens Smudge:** Human phone lenses inevitably have micro-thin lipid smudges from finger contact, generating authentic vertical/radial streak halation across light sources.
* **Auto-Exposure & Auto-Focus Breathing:** Brief 100ms micro-hunts in exposure luminance and focal depth when moving between lighting zones.
* **Biomechanical CMOS Micro-Jitter (8–12Hz):** Natural micro-tremors from single-hand muscular grip, eliminating mechanical gimbal floating.
* **24mm–28mm Selfie Barrel Geometry:** Natural facial perspective compression with subtle wide-angle barrel geometry.
* **Mandatory Prompt Syntax:**
  `"Shot on smartphone front-facing CMOS sensor (26mm wide equivalent, fixed f/2.2), natural optical barrel distortion, authentic single-hand grip micro-jitter (8-12Hz) with subtle micro-shake, tiny hand movements, slight framing drift, imperfect horizon, small operator corrections, natural focus breathing, slight exposure adjustment, and tiny changes in camera distance (0% gimbal stabilization, 0% tripod stability, 0% cinematic dolly movement), faint vertical streak halation from natural fingerprint grease smudges on the camera glass."`

* **Laughter Breath Leaks:** Chuckles leaking mid-word when recounting amusing or shocking moments.
* **Mandatory Prompt Syntax:**
  `"Delivering native unscripted conversational dialogue with authentic speech repairs, spontaneous micro-hesitations, quick pre-speech inhalations, and a subtle breathy chuckle mid-phrase."`
