## 🔬 SECTION III: SMARTPHONE OPTICAL ARTIFACTS & REAL-WORLD FLAWS

Real mobile footage NEVER looks like pristine cinema glass. Authentic UGC prompts MUST incorporate biological and physical phone flaws:

### 1. Greasy Fingerprint Lens Smudge & Streak Halation:
* Smartphone lenses are constantly touched by human fingers, leaving microscopic lipid oils on the glass.
* **Optical Effect:** Faint vertical or radial streak halation blooming softly across naked light bulbs, sodium streetlamps, and direct sun highlights.
* **Prompt Syntax:** `"Faint vertical streak halation and soft bloom around specular light sources from natural finger grease smudges on the smartphone camera cover glass."`

### 2. Auto-Focus Hunting & Focus Breathing:
* Real mobile cameras use phase-detection autofocus (PDAF) that briefly recalibrates when objects enter or exit the foreground.
* **Optical Effect:** A subtle 80ms–150ms focal micro-hunt when a creator raises a product or brings their hand toward the camera.
* **Prompt Syntax:** `"Natural smartphone autofocus breathing: subtle 100ms micro-hunt as creator raises product into foreground before snapping crisp sharp focus on product label."`

### 3. Dynamic Auto-Exposure Sensor Hunting:
* Smartphone ISP dynamically adjusts exposure compensation when light changes.
* **Optical Effect:** Brief, organic 0.2s exposure recalibration when moving from shade to direct sunlight or when bright car headlights sweep past.
* **Prompt Syntax:** `"Subtle smartphone auto-exposure hunting adjusting luminance smoothly across changing ambient lighting."`

### 4. Internal Lens Reflections (Mobile Flare Dots):
* Multi-element mobile lens stacks create tiny greenish or bluish ghost reflection dots opposite intense point light sources (streetlights or sun).

## 🚫🎥 SECTION IV: THE HANDHELD IMPERFECTIONS LAW (ANTI-GIMBAL / ANTI-TRIPOD)

> [!CAUTION]
> ### 🛑 STRICT ZERO TOLERANCE: EMBARGO ON STUDIO STABILIZATION
> Real UGC loses 100% of its viral credibility when it looks mechanically stabilized or mounted on professional Hollywood rigs.
> 
> * ❌ **STRICTLY FORBIDDEN:**
>   - Gimbal stabilization (DJI Ronin / smooth mechanical floating)
>   - Tripod lock-off (static robotic framing)
>   - Cinematic dolly tracks, jib arms, technocranes
>   - Steadicam floating glide
> 
> * ✅ **MANDATORY HANDHELD IMPERFECTIONS SUITE:**
>   1. **Subtle micro-shake:** Biomechanical hand tremors and muscular micro-jitters (8–12Hz).
>   2. **Tiny hand movements:** Unavoidable involuntary micro-twitches while gripping the phone.
>   3. **Slight framing drift:** Slow wandering frame due to operator posture and breathing.
>   4. **Imperfect horizon:** Natural 1°–3° canted/uneven horizon line, never mechanically level.
>   5. **Small operator corrections:** Belated micro-adjustments to re-center the subject after a drift.
>   6. **Natural focus breathing:** Organic focal micro-hunting when depth changes.
>   7. **Slight exposure adjustment:** Subtle auto-exposure sensor hunting responding to light fluctuations.
>   8. **Tiny changes in camera distance:** Operator swaying slightly forward or backward during the take.
