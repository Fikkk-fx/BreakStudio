> [!IMPORTANT]
> ### 🏛️ THE DIALOGUE COMPOSITION & RAPID-FIRE AXIOM
> Real human speech is never an unbroken drone of words. Even when a character speaks rapidly (*"nyerocos"*, panic rant, fast interrogation, comedy farce), speech naturally clusters into **RHYTHMIC BREATH PULSES (1 to 4 words per burst separated by 80ms micro-gasps)**.
> 
> When directing dialogue of any length or speed, the AI Director must orchestrate the **5 Master Cadence Archetypes**:
>
> $$\text{Rapid-Fire Staccato} \mid \text{Monosyllabic Sniper} \mid \text{Interrupted Collision} \mid \text{Staggered Baton Relay} \mid \text{Expiratory Ramp}$$
