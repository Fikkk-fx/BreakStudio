## 🎙️ SECTION I: THE UNIVERSAL SPEECH-RATE BUDGET & SYNTAX RULES

1. **SPEECH-RATE BUDGET:**
   - **Universal Default:** Maximum **2.0 words per second** of spoken duration.
   - *Emergency Exception:* Urgent Staccato / high-panic bursts may reach **2.5 words per second** for a maximum of 3.0 seconds, returning to <=2.0 wps in subsequent lines.
   - Formula: `Spoken Window Duration (s) = Word Count / 2.0`.

2. **INLINE DIALOGUE SYNTAX (PROSE & ACTING BLOCKS):**
   - In Block 1 `[PROSE]`: Every spoken line is declared organically with rich cultural dialect and register anchors:
     `speaking in native fluent [Cultural Dialect / Register] with [cadence]: "..."`
     (e.g., `speaking in natural conversational Jakarta Indonesian: "..."` or `speaking in authentic Javanese-accented Indonesian: "..."`; strictly without bracketed time tags on speech to allow natural audio cadence).
   - In Block 2/3 `[ACTING & VISAGE]`: Explicitly detail the character's facial expression, motivated eyeline, and natural mouth articulation during speech.

3. **NATURAL AUDIO FLOW & CONTINUOUS KINETIC MOMENTUM:**
   - Spoken dialogue concludes naturally with authentic vocal decay, while primary physical action, room tone, and environmental foley continue seamlessly through the final frame.

4. **VOICEOVER (VO) WPS MATHEMATICAL CALCULATION & PACING SYNCHRONIZATION LAW:**
   - Whenever Voiceover (VO), Narration, or Internal Monologue is utilized, calculating the exact **Words Per Second (WPS)** is **MANDATORY**:
     $$\text{WPS} = \frac{\text{Jumlah Kata Eksak}}{\Delta t (\text{Waktu Selesai} - \text{Waktu Mulai})}$$
   - **Calibrated Speed Envelopes (Anti-Desync Gate):**
     * *Contemplative / Somatic / Dramatic Narration:* **1.3 – 1.6 WPS**
     * *Standard Cinematic / Documentary / Storytelling Baseline:* **1.7 – 2.0 WPS (Sweet Spot)**
     * *High-Energy Hook / Commercial Trailer Narration:* **2.1 – 2.4 WPS (Safe Ceiling)**
   - ❌ **STRICT EMBARGO:** Dilarang $< 1.3\text{ WPS}$ (terlalu lambat, visual kehilangan ritme/dragging) dan dilarang $> 2.4\text{ WPS}$ (terlalu cepat, audio terburu-buru/artikulasi terpotong).
   - **Mandatory Declaration Syntax:**
     `[VO: [Speaker] — Off-Screen / Non-Diegetic | Start.s–End.s (Duration: X.Xs) | Word Count: N words | Speech Rate: X.X WPS (Target Sync: 1.7–2.0 WPS, Synchronized)]: "..."`
   - **Lip-Sync Isolation Mandate:** When a character appears on screen while VO audio plays, the character's facial kinematics MUST be locked to neutral rest (`lips closed, 0% mouth moving for VO`).

## 🏓 SECTION II: THE 25 MASTER CINEMATIC DIALOGUE ARCHETYPES (ANTI-PINGPONG CODEX)

To eliminate the amateur "Ping-Pong Tennis Match" syndrome (Person A speaks 1 sentence -> [Cut] -> Person B replies 1 sentence), the Director MUST dynamically rotate among the **25 Master Dialogue Archetypes**:

### 🏛️ DEPARTMENT A: PSYCHOLOGICAL PRESSURE & SUBTEXT
1. **Monologue Pressure vs Mute Stare:** Speaker A delivers 2-3 relentless sentences; Speaker B speaks **0% words**, maintaining an icy unblinking stare, swallowing hard, and clenching knuckles (*iceberg subtext*).
2. **Iceberg Unspoken Subtext:** Characters converse casually about mundane topics (coffee temperature, wristwatches) while the unspoken subtext conveys a deadly ultimatum.
3. **The Hesitant False-Start:** Character parts lips at 2.0s to speak, hesitates, looks down in guilt, and at 4.2s whispers 3 heavy words: *"Forgive... my departure."*
4. **Intimate Whispered Secret:** Character brings lips 10cm from listener's ear, whispering in a raspy 40dB conspiratorial tone.
5. **Sarcastic Echo & Weaponized Paraphrase:** Character repeats the opponent's exact words with cynical inflection and a cold smirk.

### ☕ DEPARTMENT B: PHYSICAL BUSINESS & PROP INTEGRATION
6. **Physical Business Parallel:** Character speaks **WHILE ENGAGED IN A PHYSICAL TASK**: flicking a Zippo lighter, stirring hot coffee, wiping a bloodstained blade, or reloading a firearm magazine. Gaze alternates between object and opponent.
7. **Walking Tracking Cadence:** Both characters walk swiftly side-by-side down a wet corridor; the camera tracks backward capturing rhythmic footsteps and brisk debate (Aaron Sorkin walk-and-talk).
8. **Back-Turned Delivery:** Character speaks while staring at rain streaks on a windowpane, facing 180° away from the listener.
9. **Invasive Spatial Intrusion:** Character steps forward aggressively into the listener's 20cm personal space while delivering a quiet threat.
10. **Object-Mediated Handover:** Character speaks while tossing a dossier onto the table, sliding a teacup, or dropping car keys on marble.

### ⚡ DEPARTMENT C: VOCAL PACING & INTERRUPTIONS
11. **Mid-Sentence Overlap Cut:** Speaker A is cut off mid-sentence: *"I only wanted to explain—"*; Speaker B instantly cuts in at 3.5s: *"—Enough! Do not speak that name!"*.
12. **Rapid Staccato Crossfire:** High-velocity banter with 0.2-second rapid pauses between exchanges.
13. **Slow Drawl Hypnotic Lull:** Deep, deliberate, hypnotic vocal cadence with extended vowel duration.
14. **Breathless Post-Trauma Gasp:** Fragmented speech interspersed with heavy chest heaves following intense physical struggle or sprint.
15. **Sudden Volume Spike Explosion:** Calm, quiet whisper suddenly erupts into a thunderous yell on a single pivotal keyword.

### 🚪 DEPARTMENT D: SPATIAL OPTICS & ACOUSTICS
16. **Off-Screen Dialogue Lead:** Speaker B's voice enters at 2.0s from off-frame; the camera holds on Speaker A's startled reaction before Speaker B enters through the doorway at 4.8s.
17. **Reaction-First Holding Frame (L-Cut):** While Speaker A is talking, the camera [HARD CUTS] to an ECU of **Speaker B listening**, capturing jaw clenching (masseter clench) and pupil dilation as the words land.
18. **Through-Glass Acoustic Muffle:** Dialogue spoken behind thick glass or a closed door, filtered with an 800Hz low-pass acoustic muffle.
19. **Three-Way Triangle Crossfire:** Three-way debate across a conference table with dynamic triangular eyeline vectors.
20. **Echoing Vault Reverberation:** Dialogue in a vaulted cathedral or hollow hangar featuring a majestic 2.5s Room Impulse Response (RIR).

### 🌐 DEPARTMENT E: CULTURAL & SOMATIC SIGNALS
21. **Code-Switching Dialect Slip:** Character abruptly shifts from formal speech to a native regional dialect or street slang when pushed to emotional boiling point.
22. **Masked Politeness / Fatal Courtesy:** Exquisitely polite aristocratic composure maintained seconds before a lethal strike.
23. **Gaze-Aversion Deception:** Character utters a falsehood while averting gaze 45° toward the floor or adjusting clothing.
24. **Laryngeal Swallow Pre-Speech Beat:** Neck muscles and Adam's apple execute a 100ms swallow in tense silence before speech begins.
25. **Unfinished Sentence Horizon Fade:** The sentence trails off into silence (*"If only back then..."*), as gaze drifts toward the distant horizon.

## 🎭 SECTION III: THE ASYMMETRICAL VOCAL DISTRIBUTION & ORGANIC ENSEMBLE REALISM CODEX

**ROOT CAUSE OF THE 'SCRIPTED/THEATRICAL' SYNDROME:**
When 3–8 characters are present in a 10–15s clip, forcing every character to take turns speaking 1 equal line (*Person A ➔ Person B ➔ Person C ➔ Person D*) creates a **fake, staged, robotic script**. In real cinema, speech is deeply asymmetrical and driven by physical survival friction.

### 🏛️ THE 5 LAWS OF ORGANIC ASYMMETRICAL ENSEMBLE SPEECH:

1. ⚖️ **THE DYNAMIC VOCAL QUOTA CEILING (UP TO 4 ACTIVE SPEAKERS PER 15s CLIP):**
   - In any single 10–15s video clip with 3–8+ characters, **UP TO 4 CHARACTERS ARE PERMITTED TO SPEAK ACTIVE SPOKEN DIALOGUE** (e.g. 1 to 4 active speakers max, enabling rapid squad callouts, tactical CQB reporting, courtroom debates, heist coordination, or multi-party family drama).
   - **Staggered Baton Relay:** Speakers deliver non-overlapping bursts (each <= 7 words max) in clear succession so AI audio synthesis remains 100% clean without chaotic vocal pile-ups.
   - Any remaining ensemble members (beyond the active speakers) communicate entirely through somatic physical action (bracing furniture, weapon aim, rapid chest heaving, scanning shadows).

2. 🔨 **ACTION-PREOCCUPIED MUTE ACTORS (PHYSICAL EXERTION SILENCE):**
   - A character exerting heavy physical force (e.g., ramming a 60kg oak desk, holding a door, sprinting, aiming a rifle) **CANNOT DELIVER COMPLETE POLISHED SENTENCES**.
   - Their jaw is clamped tight (masseter clench), mouth breathing hard (AU25/26), producing 0% words or at most a single primal grunt.

3. 💥 **THE GRADUATED 4-SPEAKER ASYMMETRIC BATON RELAY:**
   - **Primary Speaker (Leader/Commander):** Delivers the heaviest burst (3–7 words, e.g., a tactical order or urgent warning).
   - **Secondary Speaker (Responder/Specialist):** Delivers a mid-weight responsive burst (2–5 words, e.g., a status report or objection).
   - **Tertiary Speaker (Support/Lookout, if active):** Delivers a short fragmented burst (1–3 words, e.g., a one-word alert *"Atas!"* or a gasped confirmation *"Sudah terkunci!"*).
   - **Quaternary Speaker (Minimal, if active):** Delivers a single ultra-short exclamation (1–2 words, e.g., *"Awas!"* or *"Clear!"*).
   - **Remaining Ensemble (Beyond active speakers):** Strictly **0 words (100% silent somatic reaction)** — bracing furniture, weapon aim, rapid chest heaving, scanning shadows.

4. ✂️ **BROKEN PANIC SYNTAX (0% FORMAL COMPLETE GRAMMAR):**
   - In high-tension crisis scenes, humans do NOT speak in complete subject-predicate textbook grammar.
   - ❌ **SCRIPTED/THEATRICAL:** `"Dia telah menjebol ventilasi di bagian atas ruangan kita!"`
   - ✅ **CINEMA VERITE REALISM:** `"Atas!... Di ventilasi!"`

5. 👁️ **SILENT SOMATIC REACTION CUTAWAYS (L-CUTS):**
   - The camera frequently cuts to characters who **NEVER SPEAK A SINGLE WORD**, capturing their psychological processing through pure somatic cinema (trembling hands, swallowing dryly, scanning the shadows).

## 〰️🌊 THE INTRA-SENTENCE NON-LINEAR DYNAMIC WAVE CODEX (PITCH & TEMPO OSCILLATION SSOT)

> [!CAUTION]
> ### 🛑 TOP-PRIORITY PROSODY DIRECTIVE: INTRA-SENTENCE PITCH, TEMPO & VOLUME OSCILLATION!
> **HUMAN SPEECH WITHIN A SINGLE SENTENCE IS NEVER MONOTONIC OR LINEAR. IT NATURALLY RISES, DIPS, SNAPS, AND DRIFTS IN DYNAMIC WAVE CONTOURS!**

> * **1. The 4 Intra-Sentence Dynamic Wave Archetypes:**
>   - ⛰️ **Wave A: The Climax-Collapse Wave (Low ➔ High Apex ➔ Low Decay):**

>   - ⚡ **Wave B: The Rebound Snap Wave (High Shout ➔ Low Tension ➔ High Snap):**
>     - Opens with a sharp tactical shout $\rightarrow$ drops into a low suppressed throat rasp $\rightarrow$ snaps violently back up into an urgent bark.
