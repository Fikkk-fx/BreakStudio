## ⚡ SECTION III: THE 7 GOLDEN RULES OF THE REFRAME ARCHITECTURE

### 1. The `[HARD CUT: REFRAME]` Law
* In social UGC and street documentaries, editorial pacing relies on dynamic reframing (jump-cutting with lens or angle shifts) while maintaining uninterrupted continuous reality.
* Every cut must be explicitly designated using `[HARD CUT: REFRAME]`.
* Each reframe shot MUST restate the `CHARACTER LOCK: Identical [NAME]` and exact outfit/prop assignments to eliminate AI generation morphing across cuts.

### 2. The Framing Directive Scale Lock
* If the brief specifies wide/medium coverage (e.g. UGC creator showing outfit, bag, and tumbler), enforce:  
  `FRAMING DIRECTIVE: STRICTLY NO CLOSE-UP (CU) OR TIGHT FRAMING ACROSS ALL SHOTS. Every shot is framed strictly at Medium Shot (MS, waist-up) or Wide Shot (WS, full body/cowboy).`
* This prevents the video AI model from drifting into unwanted tight face-only framing.

### 3. Tactile Prop Chirality & Invariance
* Props in hand (e.g., iced coffee tumbler in right hand, canvas tote bag over left shoulder) must have **explicit hand assignments** and the keyword: `Identical across all cuts`.
* Negative locks must be included to purge common hallucinations: `0% earphone, 0% airpod, 0% heavy microphones`.

### 4. Background Pedestrian Quota (Mandate 3 SSOT)
* DILARANG menulis kerumunan tanpa kuota!
* Always specify: `Exactly N background pedestrians in motion (0% static crowd, 0% morphing)`.

### 5. Acoustic Off-Screen Interviewer Staging
* The interviewer or asking voice is staged off-screen:  
  `[Interviewer — voice only, off-screen left | X.Xs–X.Xs]: "..."`
* The interviewer's physical presence is either completely off-screen or limited to a soft OTS shoulder blur.

### 6. Natural Speech-Rate Budget & Settle Anchors (Mandates 1 & 5)
* Dialogue speech windows must not exceed 2.5 words per second.
* Every shot concludes with an explicit settle stance: `settle stance locked, eye contact steady`.
* The final shot MUST conclude with:  
  `[CLIP END — (Total - 0.8s) | 0.8s tail-buffer completely clear of dialogue/action through Total.s]`.

### 7. 100% Diegetic 5-Layer Foley Matrix
* Mandatory 5 numbered foley layers:
  1. Footwear surface impact (e.g., sneakers on paving stones).
  2. Textile micro-rustle (e.g., linen shirt in breeze).
  3. Ambient environmental base (e.g., traffic at -18dB).
  4. Handheld prop tactile sound (e.g., ice clinking in tumbler).
  5. Off-screen acoustic reflection / vocal room response.

### 8. Native Music & Dynamic Volume Hierarchy Law
* **Adaptive Musicality:** Sertakan *Native Music* instrumental yang selaras dengan mood video (misal: Lo-Fi Chill, Acoustic Beats, Synth Atmosphere).
* **Multi-Layer Subordination:** Jika terdapat beberapa layer audio (Dialog/VO + Foley + Music), volume Native Music **WAJIB LEBIH KECIL DARI AUDIO UTAMA** (level underbed -12dB s/d -18dB relative to vocal, dengan dynamic ducking -4dB s/d -6dB pada 2.5kHz).
* **Single-Layer Standard Volume:** Jika hanya ada 1 layer audio (Native Music saja tanpa suara percakapan), volume diputar pada **STANDAR PENUH (0dB Reference / -14 LUFS standard)**.

### 9. The Raw Camera Kinematics & Imperfections Mandate
* Setiap generasi video mentah/raw UGC **WAJIB** menerapkan kinematika kamera organis manusia dengan ketidaksempurnaan alami:
  ```text
  The footage must contain natural imperfections:

  •  subtle micro-shake
  •  tiny hand movements
  •  slight framing drift
  •  imperfect horizon
  •  small operator corrections
  •  natural focus breathing
  •  slight exposure adjustment
  •  tiny changes in camera distance

  Do NOT use gimbal stabilization.
  Do NOT use tripod stability.
  Do NOT use cinematic dolly movement.
  ```
  - **Detailed Micro-Physics:**
    • subtle micro-shake (biomechanical tremors 8–12Hz)
    • tiny hand movements (spontaneous finger twitches)
    • slight framing drift (respiratory torso sway)
    • imperfect horizon (natural 1°–3° canted/uneven angle)
    • small operator corrections (belated center re-adjustments)
    • natural focus breathing (organic focal hunting)
    • slight exposure adjustment (ambient auto-exposure shifts)

## 🛠️ SECTION V: BRIEF ADAPTATION PROTOCOL

When adapting this locked structure to future creative briefs:
1. **Header Adaptation:** Modify `FORMAT`, duration, aspect ratio, camera/sensor, and color grade according to the target platform and creative tone.
2. **Set & Environment:** Pull verified locations from `rules/03d_world_environment_asset_library.md` (e.g. ENV_ID codes) with strict pedestrian quotas.
3. **Character & Props:** Define character demographic, wardrobe, and handheld items with explicit hand chirality and negative locks.
4. **Shot Breakdown:** Calculate shot counts and timestamps based on dialogue pacing (≤2.5 wps), inserting `[HARD CUT: REFRAME]` between shot numbers, and preserving the final `0.8s tail-buffer`.
5. **Framing Directives:** Apply custom framing restrictions (e.g., `STRICTLY NO CLOSE-UP`, or specified framing rules) to ensure exact visual balance.
