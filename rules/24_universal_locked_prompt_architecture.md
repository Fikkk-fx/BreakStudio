## 🔒 SECTION IV: HARDWARE & INTEGRITY MANDATES (ZERO-DEFECT SSOT)

1. **The Single Structural Standard:** No other prompt format is permitted in Turn 5. All modes (Mode 1, Mode 2, Mode 3, Anime, UGC, TVC, Cinema) MUST use this 6-layer architecture.
2. **Motivated Cut Tags:** Every cut between shots must be enclosed in `━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━` with an explicit reason tag:
   - `[HARD CUT: REFRAME]` (for UGC/Interview/Vlog jump reframes)
   - `[HARD CUT: CUT-ON-ACTION]` (for momentum impact transitions)
   - `[HARD CUT: REACTION]` (for emotional listening cuts)
   - `[HARD CUT: ANGLE SHIFT]` (for spatial multi-camera coverage)
   - `[HARD CUT: LOCATION SHIFT TO [NAME]]` (for room transitions)
3. **Continuous Character Restatement:** In every single shot following a cut, `CHARACTER LOCK: Identical [NAME]` must be reiterated with key wardrobe and handheld prop tokens.
4. **The Speech-Rate, VO WPS & Settle Anchor Law (Mandates 1 & 5):**
   - On-screen dialogue speed budget: $\le 2.0$ words per second default ($\le 2.5$ wps emergency burst max 3s).
   - **MANDATORY VOICEOVER (VO) WPS CALCULATION & PACING SYNCHRONIZATION:**
     Whenever a prompt incorporates Voiceover (VO / V.O. / Narration / Internal Monologue), the prompt MUST explicitly calculate the exact Words Per Second (WPS):
     $$\text{WPS} = \frac{\text{Total Spoken Words}}{\text{Spoken Duration Window } \Delta t \text{ (s)}}$$
     - **Safe Synchronization Envelope:**
       * *Contemplative / Somatic / Dramatic Narration:* **1.3 – 1.6 WPS**
       * *Standard Cinematic / Documentary / Corporate Narration:* **1.7 – 2.0 WPS (Balanced Sweet Spot)**
       * *Urgent Action / Trailer / Fast Hook Narration:* **2.1 – 2.4 WPS (Upper Safe Ceiling)**
       * ❌ **STRICTLY FORBIDDEN:** $< 1.3\text{ WPS}$ (causes sluggish dragging and visual pacing collapse) or $> 2.4\text{ WPS}$ (causes rushed audio, truncated phonemes, chipmunk distortion, and visual desync).
     - **Anti-Mouth-Flapping Biometric Lock:** During VO, all characters visible in frame MUST be explicitly locked with: `lips closed, resting mouth position, 0% mouth flapping / on-camera articulation for VO`, preventing AI diffusion models from hallucinating false lip movement.
     - **Acoustic Ducking Alignment:** In the Audio block, foley and background sound must duck by **-4dB to -6dB at 2.5kHz** precisely matching the declared VO start-to-end seconds.
   - Settle stance mandatory on each shot before cuts.
   - Final shot mandatory closure: `[CLIP END — (Total - 0.8s) | 0.8s tail-buffer completely clear of dialogue/action through Total.s]`.
5. **100% Diegetic 5-Layer Foley Matrix (Mandate 14 Pillar 7):**
   - Every audio block must explicitly number 5 distinct physical sound layers `[1]` to `[5]`.
6. **The Native Music & Dynamic Volume Hierarchy Law (Mandate 4 & Universal Calibration):**
   - **Adaptive Native Music:** Setiap audio block WAJIB mencantumkan *Native Music* yang dikalibrasi secara harmonis mengikuti mood adegan, genre, dan tempo visual (misal: Lo-Fi Chill untuk Coffee UGC, Cinematic Pulse untuk Thriller, Piano/Acoustic untuk Drama).
   - **Multi-Layer Subordination (Volumenya Lebih Kecil dari Audio Utama):** Jika adegan memuat beberapa layer audio (Dialog Vokal On-Screen, Voiceover, atau Foley Aksi Keras), volume *Native Music* **WAJIB LEBIH KECIL DARI AUDIO UTAMA** (diturunkan ke level background underbed -12dB s/d -18dB relative terhadap level vokal 0dB reference, dengan dynamic vocal-pocket ducking -4dB s/d -6dB pada frekuensi 2.5kHz selama jendela bicara).
   - **Single-Layer Standard Volume (Volumenya Standar jika 1 Layer Saja):** Jika adegan HANYA memuat 1 layer audio (hanya Native Music saja tanpa dialog/vokal, misal B-roll murni, montage, atau visual showcase), volume Native Music dimainkan pada **STANDAR NOMINAL LENGKAP (0dB Reference / -14 LUFS standard)** tanpa ducking atau peredaman buatan.
7. **The Raw UGC Camera Kinematics & Natural Imperfections Mandate (Mod 06c/23):**
   Whenever generating prompts for Raw UGC, Social Mobile Native, Bystander Formats, or Street Interviews, the `CAMERA RIG:` layer MUST strictly include:
   ```text
   The footage must contain natural imperfections:

   •  subtle micro-shake
   •  tiny hand movements
   •  slight framing drift
   •  imperfect horizon
   •  small operator corrections
   •  natural focus breathing
   •  slight exposure adjustment
   •  tiny changes in camera distance

   Do NOT use gimbal stabilization.
   Do NOT use tripod stability.
   Do NOT use cinematic dolly movement.
   ```
