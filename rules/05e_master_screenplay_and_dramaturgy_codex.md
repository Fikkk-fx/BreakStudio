### ⚡ 1. TIER 1: THE 10-SECOND BYSTANDER & ABSURD CONTRAST ARC (1 KLIP @ 10s)
Designed for ultra-viral TikTok/Reels loops and candid "Friend-Cam" moments:
* **Cut 1 (0.0s – 2.5s) — In-Media-Res Medium-Wide Handheld Hook:**
  - Camera rolls while character is caught mid-action (eating snack, slouching on plastic stool, fumbling with keys).
  - Subject notices camera and blurts out the opening hook with natural eyebrow twitch.
* **Cut 2 (2.5s – 5.0s) — Natural Handheld Reframing (Medium Shot):**
  - Operator steps in or shifts weight; subject continues talking while gesturing with prop.
  - Brief off-camera glance toward the friend holding the phone seeking agreement.
* **Cut 3 (5.0s – 7.5s) — Genuine Reaction & Somatic Micro-Accident:**
  - Incredulous micro-laugh accompanied by a somatic mishap (dropping a crumb, tongue click, tucking hair).
* **Cut 4 (7.5s – 10.0s) — Casual Resolution & Settle Beat:**
  - Final punchline delivery finishing at 9.2s (`Total - 0.8s`).
  - Final 0.8s: Natural breathing, relaxed smile, lips locked closed, eyes shifting back to phone screen.

### 📱 2. TIER 2: THE 15-SECOND NATIVE TIKTOK/REELS ARC (1 KLIP @ 15s)
The industry standard for high-converting social media videos:
* **Beat 1 (0.0s – 3.0s): The 0.5s Visual Anomaly & Bold Spoken Hook:**
  - Frame 1 contains dynamic motion (liquid splashing, hands tearing packaging, abrupt camera tilt).
  - Bold spoken premise with immediate speech repair: *"Kalian jangan pernah... eh seriusan, jangan beli ini sebelum tahu ini..."*
* **Beat 2 (3.0s – 7.5s): Demonstrative Physical Action & Proof:**
  - Handheld camera zooms or reframes via `[HARD CUT: REFRAME]`. Subject shows texture, applies product, or shows phone screen.
  - Active natural speech with 80% screen viewfinder / 20% camera hole eye-drift.
* **Beat 3 (7.5s – 12.0s): Spontaneous Twist, Personal Vulnerability, or Banter:**
  - Unexpected sensory reaction (*"Rasanya tuh kayak... wow, dingin banget!"*), authentic chuckle, or bystander reaction.
* **Beat 4 (12.0s – 15.0s): Candid Recommendation & Settle Stance:**
  - Spoken dialogue concludes sharply at `14.2s`.
  - The final 0.8s is strictly locked: character lowers hand, breathes naturally, lips firmly closed to prevent cutoff artifacts.

### 🛍️ 3. TIER 3: THE 30-SECOND DUAL-CLIP PRODUCT & STORYTIME ARC (2 KLIPS @ 15s)
Designed for affiliate marketing, product reviews, and unscripted storytime vlogs:
* **Klip 1 (0.0s – 15.0s) — The Relatable Dilemma & First Impression:**
  - *Setup (0–5s):* Creator at desk or walking, sharing a relatable annoyance or urgent question.
  - *Development (5–12s):* Introducing the solution/product, tactile unboxing foley, honest skepticism.
  - *Cliffhanger / Transition (12–15s):* Preparing the test/demonstration. Dialogue finishes at 14.2s with active physical momentum leading into Klip 2.
* **Klip 2 (15.0s – 30.0s) — The Real-World Test & Spontaneous Verdict:**
  - *Verification (15–22s):* Immediate sensory payoff, macro texture swatch, authentic facial surprise (AU6+AU12).
  - *Unvarnished Opinion (22–27s):* Highlighting both a genuine pro and a tiny relatable quirk (adds 100% credibility).
  - *Final Outro Settle (27–30s):* Casual parting words ending at 29.2s. Final 0.8s locked in relaxed authentic smile.

### 🎙️ 4. TIER 4: THE 60-SECOND EXTENDED STREET INTERVIEW & VOX POP (4 KLIPS @ 15s)
The premier format for roving street creators, campus interviews, and public challenges:
* **Klip 1 (0.0s – 15.0s) — The Cold Approach & Shock Question:**
  - Host walking with wireless microphone stops stranger on sidewalk; stranger displays genuine initial surprise.
  - Host asks the core provocative/fun question.
* **Klip 2 (15.0s – 30.0s) — Hesitation, False Start & First Opinion:**
  - Stranger laughs nervously, glances at friend off-camera, starts answering, corrects themselves mid-phrase.
  - Host nods and reacts with motivated micro-interjections.
* **Klip 3 (30.0s – 45.0s) — The Counter-Argument & Spontaneous Debate:**
  - Host challenges stranger's answer or brings in bystander friend; playful tension and banter escalate.
* **Klip 4 (45.0s – 60.0s) — The Final Verdict, Reward & Laughing Wrap-Up:**
  - Stranger delivers definitive funny/heartfelt answer; prize or high-five exchanged; outro wraps at 59.2s with natural crowd ambiance.

## 🎭 SECTION II: THE 6 MASTER DRAMATURGICAL LAWS FOR RAW UGC

1. **The Anti-Influencer Authenticity Law:**
   - 0% canned influencer catchphrases (*"Hey guys, welcome back to my channel!"*). Start directly in mid-thought or mid-action.
2. **The Speech Repair & False Start Mandate:**
   - Dialogue must contain real human speech imperfections: mid-phrase corrections, tongue clicks, thinking pauses (*"hmm"*, *"tapi ya..."*).
3. **The 80/20 Eyeline Drift Law:**
   - In selfie mode, the creator looks 80% at their screen viewfinder and only 20% at the camera hole, with situational glances at passersby.
4. **Task-Embedded Body Kinematics:**
   - The creator must never stand still like a statue while speaking. They must be fidgeting with hair, holding a drink, walking, or unboxing a product.
5. **The 9:16 Social UI Safe-Zone Rule:**
   - All critical physical actions and facial expressions must remain inside the central 70% of the vertical frame, avoiding UI icons and captions.
6. **The Inviolable 0.8s Tail-Buffer:**
   - Every clip's speech MUST conclude at `Total Duration - 0.8s`. The remaining 0.8s guarantees zero mouth-flapping or mid-sentence cutoffs.
